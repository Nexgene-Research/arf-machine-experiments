- MN5 debug submission requires `sbatch --qos=acc_debug --partition=acc --account=ehpc926`, one debug job at a time. MN5 partition allocation through generic compute_job was unsuitable for the debug QoS route. Scripts use LF line endings; no explicit memory request.
- Giraffe 47356275 was not interrupted or used in v1 ranking. MoChA attempts failed and were not restarted; neither result supports a claim of mosaic aneuploidy.
- 2026-10-17: optional Phase E was completed after the first archive. Chromosome-arm depth from 100 kb mosdepth bins (`results/evidence/aneuploidy/arm_depth.tsv`) and GATK ModelSegments allele balance from BAM allelic counts (MN5 job 47439658, `results/evidence/aneuploidy/mosaic_segments.tsv`) replace MoChA, which cannot run on a variant-only VCF. This timing deviation was chosen to resolve critical objections before release rather than pretend the review occurred at its nominal time.

The public release omits them unchanged to satisfy its controlled-data/secret scan; the scripts remain in the authorized project, the SHA-256-verified receipt is in `results/track1/bcd_47403447_receipt.json`, and the analysis-generating MN5 `.py`/`.sbatch` files are copied unchanged. - The automated historical scoring script treated rare alleles as PM2 moderate and gene membership as PP4, without independent variant-level calibration. These are historical calculation inputs, not assertions that ACMG criteria are clinically met. New claims require criterion-specific source audit.
- The internal review raised three objections to the v2 draft: the damaging effect of p.Asn1002Lys was not demonstrated, the historical 0.1 pair score rested on automatic PM2-moderate and PP4 assignments, and an early draft wrongly described the alleles as lying in distinct phase blocks. Resolution (2026-10-17): calibrated AlphaMissense evidence (0.923, PP3_Moderate interval of Bergquist et al. 2025) and the published kinase-domain destabilisation mechanism of MVA substitutions (Suijkerbuijk et al. 2010) now support the missense, which is classified VUS pending phase and function; criteria were re-assigned at criterion level, giving a conditional pair heuristic of 0.05 (`results/evidence/ranking/epcr_criterion_audit.tsv`); the phase description was corrected (neither allele lies in a reported block). The submitted CSV is unchanged. Its pair matches the organisers' clinically confirmed answer (full rank points on the automated Track 1 scorer). A cap never raises a smaller computed value.
