1. Record sample and VCF metadata, sex and chromosome-level coverage, allelic balance, contamination/chimerism, ROH/UPD and quality before interpreting genotypes. Record missing assays rather than asserting they passed. Compare provided Sentieon/GATK calls with independent MN5 recall when delivered; investigate every discordance from reads. REF-check, decompose multiallelic records, left-align indels against the supplied GRCh38 no-alt FASTA. Never infer two trans alleles merely from `0/1` twice.
2. Keep independent A (genotype/rarity/effect across coding, splice, regulatory, CNV/SV, mosaic/non-PASS), B (phenotype only), C (mechanism only), D (published cases only) rankings blinded until convergence. Tier 1 literature-supported MVA genes BUB1B, CEP57, TRIP13, CENATAC, MAD1L1 and BUB1; Tier 2 independently connected mitotic genes; Tier 3 genome-wide. Do not treat a genotype missing from one caller as absent. For any recessive locus with one plausible allele, search UTR/promoter/deep intronic/indel/SV/CNV/low-VAF and inspect coverage and phase. Do not discard variants by a strict 0.5 VAF filter.
3. Phenotype fit weights: tumor histology and constitutional cytogenetic pattern strongest, growth restriction and congenital renal disease intermediate, perinatal outcomes supportive but nonspecific; parental miscarriages used as inherited-history signal and not counted as the child's feature. Compare blended diagnosis. Phenotype-only support cannot substitute for a verified allele.
4. Literature entries require an attributable primary source and exact assembly/normalization before entering the read-genotyping queue. Unresolved coordinate or allele is recorded as unresolved, never guessed. Align source case genotypes and tumor phenotypes with functional observations; do not assume a published allele explains this child.
5. Score alternatives including biallelic canonical loss, hypomorphic regulatory-plus-coding compound heterozygosity, monoallelic dominant/mosaic mechanism, segmental-instability pathway, artifact and blended disease. Count independent evidence sources, not mentions. A finalist needs genome REF and genotype validity, molecular-effect mechanism, gene-disease link, cellular mechanism and phenotype fit. Confidence is capped by the weakest unverified link. Red-team read-level and competing-explanation objections must be checked before a confident row 1; if evidence unavailable, lower confidence and label provisional.
6. Log every considered and rejected allele/gene and every unresolved anomaly. Resolve each high-priority anomaly by a cheapest discriminating check against at least two explicit explanations. Stop only after two rounds with stable top three and no unexplained high-priority anomaly. Until then label results interim.

## Probability and calibration policy

For each allele, map ACMG/AMP + ClinGen SVI evidence strengths into Tavtigian likelihood ratios: pathogenic very strong/strong/moderate/supporting multipliers `350, 18.7, 4.3, 2.08`; benign strong/supporting reciprocal multipliers `1/18.7, 1/2.08`. Prior pathogenic probability sensitivity grid 0.01, 0.1, 0.2; default 0.1 only for *variant pathogenicity*, not case causality. For computational evidence (AlphaGenome AVI/splicing, AlphaMissense, REVEL, ESM), determine empirical likelihood ratios with ClinVar two-star-or-better P/LP vs B/LB and no conflicting assertions, excluding all Tier 1 genes and removing duplicate alleles, with stratification by molecular consequence, allele frequency and score availability. Choose thresholds/strengths on a separate calibration split (Pejaver et al. 2022); bootstrap by gene to respect within-gene dependence. In a correlated computational family use a jointly fitted score on held-out genes if feasible; otherwise keep only the strongest validated LR from that family (never multiply correlated PP3 signals), capped at supporting until calibrated evidence warrants more. Do not impute missing prediction as benign. An uncalibrated predictor is descriptive, LR=1.

For allele `a`, `P_path(a)= logistic(logit(prior)+sum_unique_classes(log LR))`. For compound heterozygotes, use `P_pair=min(P_path(a),P_path(b))*P_trans`, with `P_trans` derived from physical or familial phase, and conservative unknown-phase probability (sensitivity 0.25–0.75; default 0.5); do not represent this as proven trans. For an individual candidate, let `q_variant=P_path` or `P_pair`; independent B/C/D and diagnostic read/QC support contribute prespecified capped log-LRs, only after real calibration, otherwise LR=1. Case-level `epcr= min(q_variant, q_phenotype, q_mechanism, q_read, q_inheritance) * A`, where A is a preregistered bounded angle multiplier `min(1, 1 + 0.1*(n_independent_angles-1))`; an absent or unverified critical link has conservative case-level confidence at most 0.1. Clip final values into (0,1] and rank by epcr with tied scores resolved by stronger read evidence then lexicographic GRCh38 key. This case-level epcr is a heuristic until case-level calibration against positive/negative controls is available, and must not be described as validated probability.

Apply the full prioritization unchanged to HG002 with this phenotype and to proband genotypes with at least 100 fixed-seed permutations of the HPO set preserving term count and broad ontology depth (if data available). For score `s` estimate empirical tail probability `(1 + count(null_max >= s))/(1 + number_of_null_genomes/permutations)` and report false candidates per genome above each threshold; no controls means p-value not estimated. Bootstrap independent evidence sources and examine both priors and criterion-strength sensitivity for epcr intervals; no interval without a completed bootstrap. A novel-gene prediction cannot be relied upon until leave-one-known-gene-disease-link-out rediscovery ranks for BUB1B, CEP57, TRIP13, CENATAC and MAD1L1 are measured without leakage; otherwise label exploratory only.
