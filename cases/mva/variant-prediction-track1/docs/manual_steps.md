# Remaining manually produced legacy artifacts

The v1 artifacts `results/track1_top10_ranked.tsv`, `results/track1/funnel.json`, and `results/exclusions.tsv` were originally partly generated with inline commands. Their primary ranked/read-stage inputs remained on MN5. A saved, remote `scripts/37_select_readbacked.py` run in Slurm job **47406904** independently reconstructed the ten-row selection from the full original ranking, without using the legacy ten-row output as input. The new `results/track1/top10_reconstructed.tsv` is byte-identical to the original (see `results/track1/legacy_reconstruction_verification_v2.json`). `scripts/38_funnel.py` now reconstructs 35 original funnel fields from upstream inputs; nine annotation/provenance fields still require earlier source artifacts, not copying the legacy funnel. `scripts/39_exclusions.py` reconstructs 17 aggregate exclusion keys, but the original reports 124 non-Tier-1 pair genes and the upstream table yields **125**, a discrepancy preserved and explained in `results/track1/legacy_reconstruction_audit.md`. Neither grouped exclusions table lists every individually rejected patient variant or reproduces the original manually worded reasons. Originals remain unchanged; the data-level gap is explicit rather than hidden.

The v2 CSV was generated by a saved script (`scripts/41_write_v2.py`) from bounded, read-supported allele evidence and passed the format validator. The fresh-context internal review questioned the evidence for the missense allele and the score; the objections and their resolution are recorded in `docs/deviations.md` and in section 6 of the report. The CSV is unchanged and is the submitted prediction. Descriptive expert adjudication is model-assisted judgment reviewed by the authors, not deterministic code.
