# Track 1 methods and decision audit

## Deterministic stages

Read handling and calling on authorized MN5 allocation: `scripts/mn5/l1_20261015_align.sbatch`, `l1_20261015_merge.sbatch`, `fastpath_run_v2.sbatch` and `critical_annotation_20261016.sbatch`; candidate filtering, pairing, read support, ePCR via `scripts/mn5/track1_scores.py`, `track1_pairs.py`, `track1_reads.py`, `track1_epcr.py`. v2 read-backed and phase checks are documented in their newly archived scripts and bounded summaries. `scripts/41_write_v2.py` records adjudication and emits the final CSV; `scripts/validate_submission.py` REF-checks and checks the template. Hashes and incomplete source receipts appear in `docs/lineage.md` and `results/provenance.tsv`.

GRCh38 normalized VCF 1-based alleles. In the preregistered ePCR heuristic, `P_path` uses unique supported ACMG criteria, `P_pair=min(P_a,P_b) × P_trans`, P_trans=0.5 for unknown phase, and unverified critical links cap case-level ePCR at 0.1. **A cap never raises a smaller computed value**: automatic historical PM2-moderate and PP4 assignments to the limiting missense are not criterion-level validated here, and with missense LR=1 the conditional default pair score is 0.05 (`scripts/53_redteam_reconcile.py`). Computational predictor scores lacking held-out calibration have LR=1; AlphaMissense and REVEL are interpreted against the published ClinGen calibrations (Bergquist et al. 2025; Pejaver et al. 2022). The submitted v2 CSV keeps its original 0.1 ranking heuristic; criterion-level classification of each allele is given in section 4 of the report. ePCR is a contest ranking heuristic, not a validated clinical probability; equal scores remain equal. v2 tie adjudication was dated after v1 and before further evidence: `results/track1/v2_adjudication_protocol.md`.

## Source retrieval and model-assisted judgments

`results/track1/literature_queries.tsv` logs only public gene symbols or single public allele notations per external request. `results/track1/literature_evidence.tsv` records source IDs, reading extent, direction and independent-source flags; `results/track1/evidence_convergence.tsv` aggregates evidence dimensions. Interpretations about molecular damage, phenotype specificity and competing explanations are human/AI-assisted judgments separately documented on candidate cards and in the jury report. They cannot be regenerated bit-for-bit from model outputs; source identifiers and resulting tables permit audit. The clinical description and restricted genome data must not be submitted to public databases or MCP tools.

## Known calibration and identifiability limits

Uncalibrated PP4 gene assignment and PM2 frequency assumptions in historical MN5 ePCR must not be interpreted as formally adjudicated ACMG classifications. No independent case-level calibration, familial segregation, RNA assay, confirmed SV/CNV or demonstrated mosaic chromosome abnormality was available in the legacy ranking. The chr20 HG002/HG003 benchmark measures that interval and callset, not the probability that any one candidate is causal. Recorded phase `unknown` remains unknown despite pairs of heterozygous genotype calls. Public source terms distinguish MVA1 (BUB1B), MVA2 (CEP57), MVA3 (TRIP13) and overlapping BUB1 phenotypes; a phenotype gene match cannot repair an unsupported allele.
